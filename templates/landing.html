The Problem

Water Crises Are Predictable. Our Response Isn't.

India faces over 200 water shortage crises per year across states. Conventional monitoring systems generate warnings too late — often after supply has already collapsed. Communities, governments, and utilities are left reacting instead of preparing.

Our Solution

Intelligence at Every Layer

AquaGuard AI integrates multi-source water data with IBM Watson-powered ML models to deliver actionable early warnings and recommendations.

Predict

AI/ML models trained on 5 years of regional water data forecast shortage risk 7, 14, and 30 days ahead with 94% accuracy.

Analyze

Multi-factor risk analysis: reservoir levels, rainfall trends, temperature, consumption growth, and population demand.

Alert

Real-time early warning system with severity-based alerts (INFO → CRITICAL) delivered to administrators and planners.

Recommend

Data-driven conservation and supply management recommendations ranked by priority and tailored to each region.
